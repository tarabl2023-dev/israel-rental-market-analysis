How the estimates work

Comfortable is the official average monthly rent from the Israel Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS) for that area and size, for . It covers new and renewed contracts.

CBS publishes averages only, not the spread of prices. The budget and luxury levels are rules of thumb, applied to the CBS average:

  • Budget: about 80% of the average (range 70-90%)
  • Comfortable: the average (range 90-115%)
  • Luxury: about 140% of the average (range 125-170%)

Treat them as a starting point and check real listings (Yad2, Madlan, Komo) in the neighbourhoods you like. Prices vary a lot within a city: north Tel Aviv and south Tel Aviv are very different markets.

Size bands follow CBS: 1-2, 2.5-3, 3.5-4 and 4.5+ rooms. The 4.5+ band covers everything from 4.5 rooms to large penthouses, so its average is less precise. CBS hides 1-2 room figures for Kfar Saba and Beit Shemesh (too few contracts). For those, the estimate uses the city's 2.5-3 room rent scaled by its district's price ratio.

Prices are estimates and exclude arnona (municipal tax), building committee fees (va'ad bayit) and utilities.

Data: Israel Central Bureau of Statistics, Price Statistics Monthly, Table 4.9. Part of the Israel Rental Market Analysis project (R & Tidyverse).
